Why a tree

A bristlecone pine records its whole life in rings — a tight ring for a hard year, a wide one for a kind one — and keeps every one for thousands of years. These projects work the same way: start from the primary public record, keep every vintage, and read the change slowly instead of reacting to the headline print.

Everything here is built from open government and public data, on free-tier infrastructure, and reasoned in the open — with its own caveats stated plainly rather than sanded off. The code and the tables behind every chart are published at github.com/bristlecone-public/bristlecone, so anything here can be checked against its source rather than taken on trust.
